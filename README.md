# Applied Statistical Methods for Public Health
*2026-04-27 11:00 PDT*

A graduate textbook covering the applied methodological
core of an MS-level biostatistics curriculum: causal
inference, longitudinal and survival analysis at applied
depth, clinical trial design and analysis, missing data,
meta-analysis, and advanced categorical methods.

## Structure

Twelve chapters in five parts:

1. **Foundations.** Estimands; epi measures and study
   design.
2. **Causal inference.** Foundations; estimation;
   mediation.
3. **Correlated data and time-to-event.** Longitudinal;
   survival applied.
4. **Clinical trials.** Design; analysis and reporting.
5. **Specialised methods.** Missing data at depth;
   meta-analysis; advanced categorical.

## Position in the series

This volume is the applied-methods axis of a six-volume
graduate biostatistics sequence:

- *R for Biostatistics: A One-Week Boot Camp* —
  pre-program preparation
- *Biostatistics Practicum* — workflow infrastructure
- *Statistical Computing in the Age of AI* —
  introductory methods and computing
- *Advanced Statistical Computing in the Age of AI* —
  advanced computing
- *Applied Generative AI for Public Health and
  Biostatistics* — generative AI as the orthogonal axis
- *Applied Statistical Methods for Public Health*
  (this volume) — applied methodological core
